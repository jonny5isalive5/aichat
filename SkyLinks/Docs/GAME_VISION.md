# SkyLinks — Keeping Friends Connected

**Keeping Friends Connected** is the game's tagline and central design thesis.

SkyLinks gives four friends a place to spend time together through a shared round of golf. Golf supplies the activity and friendly competition; conversation, company and shared moments give the round its purpose.

## What this means for the game

- Stay together socially across the whole course. Group voice continues in the lobby, between shots, while driving and on the scorecard. It is not proximity chat.
- Use open mic by default in a joined multiplayer room, with a visible, immediate microphone mute button and individual player mute controls.
- Make joining friends easy through room codes and, once the online service is configured, invitations.
- Keep waiting players part of the experience. Watching a shot and travelling between shots should leave room for conversation.
- Keep the interface calm and the course welcoming. Competition should add shared stories without getting in the way of spending time together.

## Presentation

Show **Keeping Friends Connected** beneath the SkyLinks name in the lobby. Use this exact wording in future title screens and promotional material. The tagline describes the intended experience; unfinished online or voice features must still be labelled honestly during development.
