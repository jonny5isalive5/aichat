D. DTW vs NN paired comparison on the same 270 (or 360) utterances
==============================================================================
McNemar exact test: discordant pairs b (NN right, DTW wrong) vs c (NN wrong, DTW right).
Two-sided p = 2*min tail of Bin(b+c, 1/2). Smallest (b, c=0) that reaches p < 0.05: 6 discordant all one way (p = 0.0313)
If both systems are ~93% and discordance is 8% of 270 (~22 pairs), detectable split (one-sided p<0.05): 16 vs 6  -> a difference of ~3.70% in accuracy
